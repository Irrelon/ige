Entity streaming: a host and two clients in this page, over a simulated link. Fly in 3D: W/S pitch, A/D yaw, Q/E roll, Space thrust (pad: left stick pitch/yaw, LB/RB roll, RT thrust). Left: A takes the keyboard and the first pad. Right: B takes a second pad if there is one, otherwise it flies a scripted loop. The faint outline on your own ship is the server's last state. G toggles the host's own entities (outlines).
